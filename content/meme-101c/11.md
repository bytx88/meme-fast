## Earning is not automatically abuse
A creator may fund work through disclosed fees or token sales. Check quantity, timing, depth, disclosure, and evidence of spending. Compare promises with actual delivery.

For this course, alignment means the records show work and participation supporting each other. Divergence means their interests or activity are moving apart. Destructive distribution describes sales that repeatedly overwhelm buying or involve supported evidence of concealment. Record the observable behavior before assigning a motive.

| Situation | What to check | Possible conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disclosed limited sales | Quantity, depth, bill, delivery | Spending may be supported; remaining supply still matters |
| Repeated promotion with sales | Wallet links, quantities, claims, subsequent work | Investigate recurring distribution and disclosures |
| Work continues but buyers leave | Releases, buyer activity, liquidity | Demand may be weakening despite delivery |
| Fees claimed, tokens retained | Recipients, claims, all known sale routes | Record fee income and check token holdings separately |

## How to investigate it
Compare multiple equal event windows. Record group inventory, sales, earnings, liquidity, announced work, delivery, and traceable payments. Compare sale quantities with current buying and size-specific quotes. A treasury transfer leaves the expense question open until the payment and its purpose can be supported.

## What does the result mean?
Update the buying case and supply checks separately. Record which fact changed and what evidence would change your conclusion again. A team can deliver work while buyer demand or trading conditions deteriorate.

## Check your understanding — answered
**The dev sold. Was it malicious?** Check amounts, disclosure, depth, related wallets, and use of proceeds before interpreting the pattern.

**Funds went to treasury. Is reinvestment established?** Look for the subsequent payment and evidence of its purpose.

**Price rose during sales. Are incentives healthy?** Review the quantities, remaining holdings, control, and buyer persistence.
