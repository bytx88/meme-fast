## See beyond your own position
A retail trader sees price, promotion, a scanner label, and a holder list. They may not see the full supported group, cost basis, changeable powers, next unlock, fee recipient, or undisclosed support. Ask what each tool omits before converting its summary into confidence.

Your demand thesis asks why buyers might arrive and persist. Supply hygiene asks what the creator and other holders can change, sell, or withdraw. A strong story cannot repair a failed control check; low concentration cannot create demand.

## A practical review card
| Area | Record this | Meaning for the next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and issuance | Exact chain/contract, launch mode, supply and authority | Wrong identity or unresolved issuance blocks a reliable supply conclusion |
| Inventory | Primary address, supported group, cost context, float denominator | Apply concentration to the group; mark unsupported ownership claims |
| Availability | Current unlocked holdings, next releases, change rights | Separate present pressure from future supply |
| Market access | Actual pool, position control, permissions, current route quote | Check executable conditions separately from the chart |
| Creator economics | Fee recipients, sales, treasury and verified use | Explain how support or monetization can change |
| Demand thesis | Credible exact-token catalyst, independent participation, competitors | Explain why buying might persist rather than assuming it |
| Relationship | Delivery, inventory changes, earning and buyer persistence | Reassess alignment or divergence with evidence |
| Unknowns | Missing histories, unverified links, offchain arrangements, stale data | State what prevents confidence; do not silently treat unknown as safe |

## How to investigate it — a short workflow
1. Confirm the contract, chain, platform mode, and current mint/contract controls.
2. Use the bubble map and top holders; open suspicious owners in the explorer and trace supported links.
3. Check locks, unlocks, position controls, ordinary selling evidence, and quotes.
4. Compare creator earnings and behavior with actual demand and the primary catalyst.
5. Write thesis status, hygiene status, unresolved evidence, and the condition for reassessment. Size and loss planning remain separate exposure decisions.

## Practical case — illustrative
The scanner says “Dev 2%.” Five supported related fresh wallets hold a combined 10% of the verified float. Minting is disabled, but 4% more attributed inventory becomes transferable tomorrow. The destination pool works; large-sale quotes have substantial impact. Promotion is active, while much buying comes from the supported group.

Your concentration screen has not passed. Current minting evidence is favorable but does not cancel inventory or demand concerns. Watch independent participation, actual sales, unlocks, and liquidity. Do not summarize this token as “safe because mint revoked” or “scam because bundled.”

## A useful conclusion
**“Demand thesis unresolved; supply screen fails my chosen group threshold. Current minting path is disabled, but linked sellable inventory and tomorrow's unlock matter. Creator-payment meaning is unverified. Reassess after independent buying, actual sales, and fresh quote evidence.”**

Checks reduce blind spots and help identify technical or structural risks. They cannot guarantee every relevant position is visible, eliminate technical risk, or predict market acceptance. Where a critical check is unsupported, say so.

## Check your understanding — answered
**Clean bubble map, exciting story: enough?** No. Verify the other review areas. **Technical checks pass: token will rise?** No; demand is separate. **Unexplained gap: fill it with a label?** No; keep it unresolved and specify what evidence is needed.
