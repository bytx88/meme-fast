## Look across the wallets
A scanner finds a creator or deployer from launch records. That wallet may hold only part of the group's tokens. On Solana, one owner can have several token accounts. Identify owners before adding balances.

Follow outgoing tokens and their recipients. A sale shows tokens exchanged for another asset; a transfer to an unclear recipient leaves ownership unresolved.

## What is a bundle?
In meme research, a **bundle means supply captured through coordinated genesis/very-early buying, at or near the initial price**. Genesis means creation. A group can fund several wallets for launch purchases, splitting a large holding across small wallets.

Check scanner rules: bundle % may use initial purchases or remaining holdings, different windows, and different supply totals. A technical transaction bundle groups transactions for ordered execution; its use alone proves neither common ownership nor cheap entry.

## Why is it risky?
Early buyers may profit below later buyers’ entry prices. Compare their remaining inventory with what the pool and buyers can absorb.

## Check the map and its gaps
A bubble map covers some transfers. Investigate the top ten ordinary holders and connected groups. Identify pool, burn, exchange, router, and system accounts separately.

Exchange custody, private agreements, and missing history can hide connections. No map link does not rule out coordinated owners. Keep confirmed and suspected links separately.

## Set up a concentration screen
Use **3% as an example personal limit**; record your own. Add reliably linked, available holdings. Check permissions, unlocks, liquidity, and demand too.

Calculate **linked available units ÷ available holder supply × 100**. Available holder supply means units ordinary holders can transfer now, excluding pool reserves, burned units, and locked units. Label this denominator beside the percentage.

If you cannot establish that total, record known units and the provider's percentage with its definition. Leave your available-supply percentage unresolved.

## How to investigate it
1. Copy the full token address and chain into the explorer and supported map tool. Save time and coverage.
2. Confirm the creator in the launch transaction. Open Holders and find the owner of each relevant token account.
3. Check the top ten ordinary holders: first funding, allocations or purchases, transfers, and current balances.
4. Open transaction signatures and save quantities, direction, assets received, and evidence of ownership links. Follow outgoing tokens to their current recipients.
5. Add confirmed group balances once. Keep uncertain owners and custody accounts separately. Refresh after trades and transfers.

## Practical case — illustrative
At 09:00 UTC on Day 1, five linked SAMPLE wallets hold 2m units each. Together they hold 10m of the 100m available holder units: **10%**, exceeding the example 3% limit.

At 09:10 UTC, records show creator wallet A sent allocations to those five wallets. These are allocations; this case does not establish bundled purchases or their cost. A still holds 2m, so the confirmed creator group holds **12m, or 12% of available holder units**. That is **8% of the 150m total issued units**; write which total you use. One group wallet then moves 2m to B, which the records establish is controlled by the group. The group still holds 12m; update its address list.

## Check your understanding — answered
**Each wallet is below 3%. Can I pass the screen?** Add reliably linked wallets before deciding.

**They bought in the same block or used the same exchange. Can I combine them?** Save those leads and seek stronger ownership evidence.

**The creator balance fell. What happened?** Follow outgoing transactions and received assets. Check the group's remaining holdings.

**The map has no links, or tokens arrived without payment. What next?** Check funding, map coverage, earlier history, and allocation terms. Leave ownership or purchase cost unknown where the records are incomplete.
