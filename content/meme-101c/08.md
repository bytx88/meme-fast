## Promotion is not the same as demand
Posts, boosts, KOL mentions, raids, partnerships, and product launches can bring attention. Check actual purchases to see who bought and how much. For a partnership or catalyst, open the dated primary source and confirm its connection to this token address.

The creator may pay for promotion or fund purchases. Record the evidence and check whether other buyers continue when that support stops.

## How to investigate a first-30-minute surge
1. Write the start/end of the surge in UTC and record the exact pool. Compare equal windows with the same coverage.
2. Open the largest purchase transactions. Find receiving owner addresses rather than treating the router as the buyer.
3. Check funding, earlier activity, transfers, and known creator links for those owners.
4. Compare purchases by the confirmed group with other observed buying. Keep uncertain owners separate.
5. Follow subsequent group sales and remaining balances.
6. Open claimed catalyst sources and promotion disclosures. A missing item in your feed leaves the source question open.

## What retail may not see
Private promotional payments, custody accounts, and unobserved wallets can hide incentives. A public buy may be small compared with earlier sales or transferred-in holdings. Keep gaps in the record rather than guessing why price moved.

## Practical case — illustrative
From 09:00 to 09:30 UTC, SAMPLE's confirmed creator group bought 4m units and sold 4m. These trades are already included in the 12m group balance recorded earlier. The remaining observed buyers bought 2m. Buying slows in the next equal window after the group stops.

## Keep this record
Save a demand table: window, pool, coverage, creator-group buys/sales, other observed buying, catalyst source, promotion evidence, and unknown owners. Keep units and value clearly labelled.

## Check your understanding — answered
**Price doubled without news in my feed. Who caused it?** Check transactions, liquidity changes, and primary sources. Your feed may have missed an event.

**A famous wallet bought. Is it accumulating?** Compare the purchase with previous sales, transfers, and remaining holdings.

**Many posts appeared. Did buyers increase?** Check buyer activity separately and record the limits of your sample.
