## Separate the income streams
A dev can get paid through trading fees, token sales, liquidity positions, or a product. Keep separate totals. A volume-based fee can keep paying while buyers lose money, so compare earnings with sales and buyer activity.

| Income stream | What to open | What to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Token sales | Completed swaps | Units, net proceeds, remaining holdings |
| Creator fee share | Platform rules and claims | Rule, recipient, accrued and paid amounts |
| Token fees | Program settings | Rate, cap, controller, exemptions |
| LP fees | Position details and claims | Owner, earnings, claims |
| Treasury funding | Incoming/outgoing transactions | Source, signers, destination, documented use |
| Product revenue | Available customer/financial records | Amount, period, source, missing evidence |

Check recipients. A creator share can already be included in the venue fee, so avoid counting it twice in buyer costs.

## What does Pay Dev mean?
If a platform shows Pay Dev, record the platform, exact label, token, and time. Read its current explanation and check the related transaction. This course does not assign one meaning to the labels used by Axiom, Pump, and Padre.

Keep an unexplained badge unverified. Look for an accrual, claim, or payout record and recipient. Record DEX Paid profile-payment information separately.

## How to investigate it
1. Open fee documentation for the exact platform and token mode. Save URL and date checked.
2. Find the fee recipient and any rights to change it.
3. Open a claim/payout transaction. Save signature, asset, amount, recipient, and time. Check whether the amount is accrued or actually received.
4. Compare fee entries with group token sales, keeping separate totals.
5. For claimed reinvestment, follow outgoing payments and look for bills or delivery evidence. Internal treasury movements leave the spending question open.

## Practical case — illustrative
SAMPLE's made-up rule charges 1% on $100,000 of eligible volume and allocates 30% of the fee to the creator. **$100,000 × 1% × 30% = $300.** These rates are illustrative; check a real platform's current rule before calculating.

At 09:35 UTC, $300 is accrued. A later transaction shows $200 claimed and $100 still accrued. The group still holds 12m available tokens. Save fees and holdings in separate records.

## Keep this record
Save an earnings table: mechanism, period, eligible activity, rule/source, recipient, accrued amount, paid amount, transaction, and time. Put sale proceeds and expense evidence in separate rows.

## Check your understanding — answered
**The badge says Pay Dev. What happened?** Check definition and transaction. Keep it unverified until the records explain it.

**The dev earns fees. Could it still sell?** Check its token transactions and remaining holdings.

**Funds moved to treasury. Were they spent on development?** Find the outgoing payment and evidence of its purpose.
