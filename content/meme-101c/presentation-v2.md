# 101C requirement version 2 — presentation only

The learning requirements have not changed. Version 2 changes the reading order and groups the existing thirteen topics into ten sections. It does not remove or reduce topic coverage.

## Fixed principles

- Explain the basic terms before expecting readers to use them.
- Explain why the dev and supply matter, including dumping risk.
- Explain what a bundle is, how it forms, how to investigate it, and what the tools cannot establish.
- Explain how the dev earns from fees, token sales, liquidity, or a product, and how earnings differ from buyer returns.
- Explain the dev–trader relationship: work, promotion, earnings, selling, reinvestment, delivery, and changes in demand.
- Check both demand and supply. Record evidence, timestamps, and unanswered questions.
- Cover creation, allocation, starting price, acquisition cost, linked wallets, permissions, available supply, locks, unlocks, liquidity, migration, promotion, sales, and past launches.
- Teach the mechanism, where and how to check it, what the findings mean, and the limits of the evidence. Keep practical examples and answered understanding checks visible.
- Use everyday language. Explain the action and its effect without slogans or poetic contrasts.
- Keep SAMPLE illustrative, supply denominators explicit, and 3% an example personal screen. Verify platform-specific rules instead of assigning universal meanings to labels such as Pay Dev.
