## Supply and powers are separate checks
Check tokens already held and permissions that can change supply or trading. A small creator balance can still matter if an authority allows more issuance or transfer restrictions.

For standard Solana tokens, start with mint and freeze authorities. For Token-2022 and other chains, inspect applicable extensions, contract roles, upgrades, and administrators.

| Power | What could happen | Evidence to save |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mint more units | More tokens become available | Authority, enforced cap, issuance transactions |
| Freeze accounts | Affected accounts cannot transfer while frozen | Freeze authority and account status |
| Restrict transfers | Some users or routes may be blocked | Rules, controller, exemptions |
| Change fees | Selling costs or net receipts change | Rate, cap, controller, activation rules |
| Upgrade logic | Previously checked behavior changes | Administrator and governance conditions |

## How many more can they mint?
If minting is enabled, find the enforced cap and who can use the authority. If no cap can be verified, future issuance remains unresolved. If the authority is permanently revoked under the relevant standard mechanism, record that minting path as disabled and continue checking other controls.

## How to investigate it
1. Open the mint/contract and check its owner program.
2. Open authority details. Read mint, freeze, and metadata/update labels separately. A generic Authority summary may show only one type.
3. Save each authority address or revoked status, source, and time. Use decoded mint data or a supported contract inspector for fields the summary does not expose.
4. Check relevant extensions, roles, fee settings, caps, and delays.
5. Open ordinary-wallet sales and failed transaction reasons. Record the applicable settings. A quote or simulation may change before execution.

## Check your understanding — answered
**Minting is disabled. What remains to check?** Existing holdings, other powers, unlocks, liquidity, and buyers.

**One address sold. Is that enough?** Check exemptions and recent ordinary-wallet sales under the current settings.

**The explorer does not show a freeze field. What should I write?** Mark it unverified and identify the decoded data or program rules needed.
