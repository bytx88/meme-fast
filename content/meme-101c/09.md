## Cost basis changes incentives
A holder allocated tokens, an early buyer, and a late buyer can pay very different amounts for the same quantity. Record actual acquisition cost where you can reconstruct it. The current chart value does not tell you what was paid.

## Purchases, transfers, and sales
For each transaction, record what the owner gave and received. Purchases add units for consideration, transfers move units between accounts, and completed sales produce another asset. If incoming tokens have missing history, leave their acquisition cost unknown.

## How to investigate it
1. Choose a time window and confirmed wallet group. Record opening balances and source links.
2. Open transactions and classify purchases, sales, incoming/outgoing transfers, and internal group transfers.
3. Record units and actual assets paid/received, including relevant fees. Keep an accounting convention for acquisition cost.
4. Check closing balances against opening holdings plus inflows minus outflows. Internal group transfers leave the group total unchanged.
5. Explain any gap. Pool swap summaries may miss other routes, token transfers, or wallets. Do not invent a trade to balance the table.

## Practical case — illustrative
SAMPLE's group has 12m available units at 09:40 UTC. Over the next hour it sells 5m, moves 2m between its own wallets, and buys 1m.

| Event | Group change | Running available balance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening holdings | 0 | 12m |
| Completed sales | -5m | 7m |
| Internal transfer | 0 | 7m |
| Completed purchase | +1m | 8m |
| Closing balance check | 0 | 8m |

The group has reduced its available holdings by 4m. The 1m purchase is part of that calculation. Total available holder supply stays at 100m because these trades moved tokens among holders. The group now holds 8% using that denominator. Its 20m vesting allocation remains separate.

## What does the result mean?
Compare actual sales with buying and depth in the same window. Early holders may be able to sell profitably at prices where later buyers lose money. Use received assets and known acquisition cost to estimate profit; save unknown cost separately. Quote remaining sale sizes rather than valuing the entire position at the chart price.

## Keep this record
Save the transaction ledger and closing balance check. Carry the group's 8m available units, 20m locked units, missing cost history, and next unlock into the later review.

## Check your understanding — answered
**A green buy appears. Is the group adding overall?** Total purchases, sales, and outside transfers over your stated window.

**Purchase cost recovered. Are the remaining tokens free?** They still have present value and may be difficult to sell. Record remaining value and exit quotes.

**The ledger has a gap. What next?** Check missing transactions, routes, and owners. Leave the gap unresolved until supported records explain it.
