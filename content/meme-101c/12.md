## History is evidence, not a permanent dev type
Past launches can help you check delivery, allocations, promises, and repeated sales. Verify the creator/team connection first. A common launch service or exchange funder can connect unrelated projects in a tool's results.

## Reasons each side may change
| Change | Evidence to seek | Question to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fee receipts decline | Claims and comparable trade windows | Can the team continue funding work? |
| Migration fails | Platform state, transactions, route | Is destination trading usable? |
| New creator launch | Verified deployment and related activity | Is attention or funding moving elsewhere? |
| Delivery slows | Dated promises, releases, updates | What changed in the plan? |
| Buyers rotate | Exact-contract competition and comparable activity | Does the current buying case still hold? |
| Sales continue as buying weakens | Inventory, sales, depth | Can current demand absorb distribution? |

These are questions to investigate. Inactivity or one price fall does not tell you which explanation applies.

## How to investigate it
1. Find earlier deployments through creator records and exact addresses. Record confidence in the team connection.
2. Compare promises, delivery, allocations, sales, and controls across launches. Include both successful and unsuccessful examples.
3. Compare today's buyer activity, attention, and depth over equal windows with the same coverage.
4. Verify competitors' exact contracts and primary sources. Separate original information from repeated promotion.
5. Record what would prompt another check: a missed release, new deployment, unlock, control change, or continuing decline in buying.

## Practical case — illustrative
By Day 1 evening, SAMPLE's dev is still delivering updates, but buyers are moving to another verified token in the same theme. Fee receipts and other observed buying decline. Record the weaker buying case even if the team continues working. Tomorrow's 4m unlock remains scheduled.

Previous launches show two delivered updates and one missed promise. Save all three with their sources and attribution confidence. That history informs the questions you ask today; current conditions still need checking.

## Keep this record
Save a launch-history table and reassessment list: contract, team-link evidence, promise, delivery, inventory behavior, outcome, current change, and next check time.

## Check your understanding — answered
**No post for a day: abandoned?** Check promised schedules, work, transactions, and other sources before concluding.

**A previous token did well. Is this one cleared?** Check its current controls, holdings, liquidity, and demand.

**A new ticker appeared. Has the theme renewed?** Investigate fresh participation and the actual catalyst.
