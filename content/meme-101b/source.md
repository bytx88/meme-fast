# MEME 101B — Survival & Hygiene

## Goal
Meme 101B teaches the first rule of micro-cap meme trading:

> Before asking how much a token can make, first decide whether it deserves a position at all.

This module sits after Meme 101A Foundations and before Meme 202A / 202B.

- **Meme 101A** — Understand what you are looking at.
- **Meme 101B** — Decide whether the token is safe enough and credible enough to trade.
- **Meme 202A** — Snipe: understand the early move.
- **Meme 202B** — Swing: understand the mature move.
- **Meme 303** — Position Management: convert opportunities into realized portfolio returns.

The core operating model is:

**THESIS FACTOR → HYGIENE FACTOR → POSITION → LIVE DD → CONFIRM / REJECT → MANAGE**

---

# Core Principle

Risk management starts before position sizing.

A trader must separate two different questions:

## Thesis Factor
> Why could this token run?

## Hygiene Factor
> Is there any reason this token should not be traded at all?

These two factors behave differently.

**Thesis Factor is additive.**

More good evidence can strengthen the case.

**Hygiene Factor is gating.**

One severe failure can override an excellent thesis.

Example:

**Thesis: 9/10**
**Hygiene: FAIL**

Result:

**SKIP**

---

# Why 101B Matters

Rug avoidance improves the trading system in two ways.

## 1. Improve Profitability
It removes avoidable catastrophic losses caused by:

- malicious contracts
- dev extraction
- bundle dumping
- liquidity removal
- hidden sell restrictions
- coordinated insider exits
- structural token failure

These losses can destroy otherwise good expectancy.

## 2. Enable True Position Management
Once obvious structural risk is filtered out, position size can reflect the actual setup.

Then risk becomes easier to classify:

### Snipe risk
- early momentum failure
- poor conversion of attention into capital
- bundle distribution
- weak buyer acceleration
- bad execution

### Swing risk
- base failure
- distribution
- weak holder rotation
- poor absorption
- demand deterioration

### Rug risk
Handled before the trade deserves normal position sizing.

# The Three Ways a Meme Trade Dies

Meme 101B separates failure into three categories.

---

# 101B.1 — Immediate Rug

## What is it?
An immediate rug is a rapid structural failure where the token becomes untradeable or collapses because of malicious or highly concentrated control.

## Typical causes
- liquidity removed
- honeypot behavior
- sell restrictions
- malicious minting
- blacklist or freeze abuse
- creator dumping concentrated supply
- coordinated insider exit
- extreme bundle concentration
- linked dev wallets unloading simultaneously

## Key feature
The token never had a fair chance to trade normally.

## What should I check?
- can ordinary wallets buy and sell?
- who controls liquidity?
- can more supply be minted?
- can wallets be frozen or blacklisted?
- how much supply does the creator control?
- how much do linked wallets control?
- how much supply is bundled?
- how concentrated are snipers?
- are funding links suspicious?

## Beginner mistake
Looking only at upside before checking whether the token is structurally tradable.

## Takeaway
**Immediate-rug risk belongs in the pre-entry filter.**

---

# 101B.2 — Slow Rug

A slow rug is a gradual destruction of value.

It has two forms.

---

## 101B.2A — Planned Slow Rug

## What is it?
Value is extracted deliberately over time instead of through one instant dump.

## Typical behaviors
- bundle wallets distribute slowly
- creator routes tokens through fresh wallets
- linked wallets take turns selling
- liquidity is drained gradually
- repeated pump-and-distribute cycles
- insiders sell into every new wave of attention
- wallet relationships are hidden through transfers

## Why does it matter?
The chart may look alive for hours, days, or weeks while supply is continuously being extracted.

## What should I check?
- declining linked-wallet balances
- transfers into fresh wallets
- repeated sells after attention spikes
- price failing despite strong buying
- wallet clusters that appear unrelated but share funding
- liquidity deterioration

## Takeaway
**A planned slow rug hides extraction inside normal-looking market activity.**

---

## 101B.2B — Organic Slow Death

## What is it?
A token can die without malicious intent.

The narrative fades, the meta moves, attention disappears, and new buyers stop arriving.

Existing holders may remain committed, but holders alone do not move price.

## Typical sequence

**Narrative weakens**
↓
**Attention declines**
↓
**New buyers slow**
↓
**Old holders remain**
↓
**Every bounce becomes exit liquidity**
↓
**Liquidity and activity decay**
↓
**Price slowly dies**

## Why does it matter?
A dead token is not automatically a scam.

Sometimes the market simply moves elsewhere.

## What should I check?
- social activity trend
- unique buyer trend
- holder growth
- buy velocity
- new-wallet arrivals
- KOL activity
- narrative relevance
- whether rebounds attract fresh demand

## Beginner mistake
Assuming loyal holders alone can keep price alive.

## Takeaway
**Holders preserve supply; new demand moves price.**

---

# 101B.3 — Self Rug

## What is it?
A self rug happens when the trader turns a manageable trade into a catastrophic loss through poor position management.

## Common self-rug behaviors
- entering too large
- chasing vertical candles
- averaging down without a plan
- refusing to exit after invalidation
- using excessive leverage
- taking no profit after a major move
- mistaking sunk cost for conviction
- ignoring distribution
- ignoring deteriorating liquidity
- holding because the community is still loud

## Why does it matter?
The token may remain fully tradable.

The trader destroys the position through bad decisions.

## Takeaway
**A clean token can still become a bad trade through poor position management.**

Detailed position management belongs in **Meme 303**.

# Thesis Factor

## What is it?
Thesis Factor measures why the token could attract more demand.

It answers:

> Why should this token run?

## Core Thesis components

### 1. Narrative Strength
- easy to understand
- memorable
- emotionally or culturally relevant
- shareable
- distinctive enough to spread

### 2. Meta Fit
- aligned with current market attention
- related to a hot chain, theme, event, product, person, or meme
- timely rather than stale

### 3. Attention Growth
- mentions increasing
- engagement increasing
- new accounts discovering the token
- new communities joining

### 4. X Quality
- credible account
- coherent history
- authentic interaction
- real replies
- low bot-spam ratio

### 5. Website / Product Quality
- credible presentation
- working site
- coherent story
- useful links
- consistent branding
- no obvious fake claims

### 6. Community Quality
- active discussion
- real participants
- independent voices
- low copy-paste behavior
- sustained activity after price pauses

### 7. Buyer Growth
- unique buyers increasing
- buyer frequency increasing
- average buy size increasing
- repeat buyers appearing

### 8. Wallet Quality
- proven traders appearing
- returning buyers
- smart-money participation
- independent funding sources

### 9. Momentum / Acceleration
- transaction frequency increasing
- buys arriving faster
- sell size decreasing
- holder growth accelerating
- social attention accelerating

---

# Hygiene Factor

## What is it?
Hygiene Factor measures whether the token is safe enough to trade.

It answers:

> Is there any reason I should refuse this trade?

## Core Hygiene components

### 1. Dev Wallet
- current dev holdings
- dev cost basis
- dev selling history
- transfers to fresh wallets

### 2. Dev-Linked Wallets
- common funding
- repeated transfers
- coordinated entries
- coordinated exits

### 3. Bundle Concentration
- bundle percentage
- entry timing
- bundle cost basis
- remaining bundle holdings
- bundle distribution behavior

### 4. Sniper Concentration
- early sniper ownership
- sniper cost basis
- amount already sold
- remaining inventory

### 5. Funding Clusters
- multiple wallets funded from the same source
- synchronized funding
- synchronized order sizes
- circular transfers

### 6. Liquidity Safety
- who controls LP
- whether LP is locked or burned
- lock duration
- pool depth
- whether liquidity is being removed

### 7. Sellability
- ordinary wallets can sell
- no unusual transfer restrictions
- no extreme sell tax
- no blacklist abuse

### 8. Mint / Freeze / Blacklist Risk
- mint authority
- freeze authority
- blacklist control
- privileged contract controls

### 9. Contract / Token Anomalies
- suspicious taxes
- unusual transfer rules
- hidden permissions
- unexplained supply changes

### 10. Known Drainer / Exploit Behavior
- linked malicious wallets
- suspicious historical deployments
- repeated token-launch patterns
- abnormal fund routing

---

# Thesis Factor vs Hygiene Factor

| Factor | Main Question | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis Factor | Why can this run? | Additive |
| Hygiene Factor | Can this kill the trade? | Gating |

## Important rule

**Strong Thesis + Failed Hygiene = SKIP**

No narrative is strong enough to compensate for a severe structural failure.

---

# Attention Life Cycle

Micro-cap memes are usually traded through an attention lifecycle.

A useful model is:

**START → BURST → SUSTAINED → PLATEAU → RENEWAL or DOWNTREND → DRAINED**

---

## Start
The narrative is just appearing.

Question:

> Is this real or noise?

---

## Burst
Attention accelerates quickly.

Question:

> Is this becoming a trade now?

---

## Sustained
New buyers and attention keep arriving.

Question:

> Can this keep expanding?

---

## Plateau
Attention remains high but stops accelerating.

Question:

> Is this consolidation, distribution, or simply exhaustion?

---

## Renewal
A new catalyst creates another reason for fresh buyers to arrive.

Examples:
- migration
- DEX traction
- KOL attention
- new product
- ecosystem event
- new narrative extension

Question:

> Has the life cycle extended?

---

## Downtrend
Attention and new capital decline.

Question:

> Is the meta fading?

---

## Drained
Little fresh attention remains.

Question:

> Who is left to buy?

---

# Why Attention Duration Matters

Experienced traders often compress many signals into one practical judgment:

> How large is this narrative, and how long can it hold attention?

A token may only deserve:

- one 180-second bar
- a few minutes
- several hours
- a few days
- a two-week meta

The expected duration of attention should influence the expected duration of the trade.

A useful principle is:

**Position duration should follow attention duration.**

A 3-minute narrative should not automatically become a 2-week hold.

---

# Catalyst Completion Risk

Even a successful event can mark the top.

Example:

**Pre-migration**
→ attention builds

**Migration**
→ event completes

**Post-migration**
→ no new reason to buy

The token may then move:

**Start → Burst → Sustained → Plateau → Downtrend**

The event itself does not need to fail.

Sometimes the market simply runs out of the next reason to buy.

## Takeaway
**A completed catalyst can become sell-the-news if attention is not renewed.**

# Snipe Use of 101B

Snipe decisions happen quickly.

The trader cannot always complete deep due diligence before the opportunity moves.

So the process is staged.

## Stage 1 — Scan
Assess:
- narrative
- attention
- website
- X
- early buyer activity
- wallet structure

## Stage 2 — Thesis Factor
Ask:

> Is there enough reason this token could run?

If no:

**WATCH or SKIP**

If yes:

Move to Hygiene.

## Stage 3 — Hygiene Gate
Ask:

> Is there anything that can immediately destroy the trade?

Check:
- dev
- dev-linked wallets
- bundles
- snipers
- liquidity
- sellability
- mint/freeze controls
- suspicious funding

If severe risk appears:

**SKIP**

If acceptable:

The token becomes an entry candidate.

## Stage 4 — Controlled Initial Position
The logic is:

**Enough information to enter. More information to stay.**

## Stage 5 — Live DD
Continue checking:
- news
- X
- website
- wallets
- buyer flow
- holder growth
- bundle behavior

## Stage 6 — Confirm or Reject
If evidence strengthens:

**HOLD / ADD within plan**

If evidence weakens:

**REDUCE / EXIT**

# Swing Use of 101B

Swing traders have more history available.

The key question changes from:

> Can this immediately rug me?

to:

> Has this token demonstrated healthy behavior since launch?

## Historical Hygiene
Check:
- has the dev been selling?
- have linked wallets been distributing?
- has bundle concentration fallen naturally?
- has liquidity remained stable?
- have large holders already extracted most of their cost?
- does every pump become exit liquidity?
- is holder growth still real?
- has contract behavior remained stable?

## Thesis Persistence
For Swing, Thesis Factor becomes less about raw potential and more about persistence.

Ask:
- did the narrative survive the first hype cycle?
- are people still discussing it when price is quiet?
- is the meta still relevant?
- are fresh buyers still arriving?
- are important wallets returning?
- is attention being renewed?

A useful distinction:

**Snipe Thesis = potential**

**Swing Thesis = persistence**

---

# Decision States

Use a small number of clear execution states.

## SKIP
Hygiene failure or no credible thesis.

## WATCH
Interesting thesis, but not enough confirmation.

## PROBE
Thesis sufficient and Hygiene acceptable.

Small initial position.

## ADD / HOLD
Live DD confirms the original thesis.

## REDUCE / EXIT
Thesis weakens or Hygiene deteriorates.

---

# Core 101B Questions

Before entering, ask:

1. Why could this token run?
2. Is the narrative timely?
3. Is attention actually growing?
4. Is X activity credible?
5. Is the website or product coherent?
6. Who controls the dev-linked supply?
7. How much supply is bundled?
8. How concentrated are snipers?
9. Can normal wallets sell?
10. Who controls liquidity?
11. Can supply be minted or accounts frozen?
12. Is there any severe Hygiene failure?

After entering, ask:

13. Is the thesis becoming stronger?
14. Is attention converting into buyers?
15. Are quality wallets joining?
16. Are bundles holding or distributing?
17. Are large sells being absorbed?
18. Is liquidity improving or deteriorating?
19. Is this becoming a planned slow rug?
20. Is the meta simply dying?
21. Am I managing the trade, or self-rugging?
