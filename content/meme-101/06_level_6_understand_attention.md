# MEME 101 — Level 6: Understand Attention

## Goal
Understand why people notice a meme token and how attention turns into — or fails to turn into — demand.

---

## 1. Narrative

### What is it?
A narrative is the story or theme that gives traders a reason to notice the token.

### Why does it matter?
Meme tokens often compete for attention more than traditional fundamentals.

### What should I check?
- Is the story easy to understand?
- Is it timely?
- Is it unique?
- Can people repeat it quickly?
- Is attention growing beyond the original community?

### Beginner mistake
Confusing a good story with guaranteed price appreciation.

### Takeaway
**Narrative creates attention; buyers still have to convert that attention into demand.**

---

## 2. Catalyst

### What is it?
A catalyst is an event that can increase attention or demand.

### Examples
- Exchange listing
- Product launch
- Chain announcement
- Major account mention
- Community event

### What should I check?
- Whether the catalyst is confirmed
- Whether it is already priced in
- Whether new users can actually act on it

### Beginner mistake
Buying only because a date or announcement exists.

### Takeaway
**A catalyst matters only if it changes attention, access, or demand.**

---

## 3. Social Momentum

### What is it?
Social momentum is the rate at which discussion and participation are growing.

### Why does it matter?
A meme often needs continuously expanding attention to sustain high valuations.

### What should I check?
- Number of independent accounts discussing it
- Engagement quality
- Growth over time
- New communities joining
- Whether discussion survives price weakness

### Beginner mistake
Counting posts without checking whether they come from the same small group.

### Takeaway
**Broadening participation matters more than raw post count.**

---

## 4. CT / KOL Shilling

### What is it?
Crypto Twitter or KOL shilling happens when influential accounts promote or discuss a token.

### Why does it matter?
Influencers can create rapid inflows, but those inflows can reverse just as quickly.

### What should I check?
- When the KOL entered
- Whether they disclosed holdings
- Whether they are still holding
- Whether multiple KOLs are connected
- How late you are relative to their entry

### Beginner mistake
Assuming a KOL mention creates permanent demand.

### Takeaway
**Influence can create attention faster than liquidity can absorb exits.**

---

## 5. Attention Rotation

### What is it?
Attention rotation is the movement of trader interest from one meme, theme, chain, or sector to another.

### Why does it matter?
A good token can stagnate if the market's attention moves elsewhere.

### What should I check?
- Which themes are gaining volume
- Which chains are gaining meme activity
- Whether previous leaders are losing participation
- Whether capital is rotating into new launches

### Beginner mistake
Evaluating a token without considering what the rest of the meme market is doing.

### Takeaway
**Meme performance is partly relative: attention has alternatives.**

---

## 6. Chain Narrative

### What is it?
A chain narrative is the broader story around the blockchain where the meme trades.

### Why does it matter?
A chain experiencing strong user growth, new products, or speculative interest can lift its meme ecosystem.

### What should I check?
- Chain activity
- DEX volume
- Launchpad activity
- New users
- Ecosystem catalysts

### Beginner mistake
Assuming every token on a hot chain will benefit equally.

### Takeaway
**A strong chain can provide wind at the token's back, but it does not remove token-specific risk.**

---

## 7. Meme Lifecycle

### What is it?
A meme lifecycle describes how attention and ownership often evolve.

### Common path
Launch → discovery → expansion → distribution → continuation or death.

### Why does it matter?
The same chart can mean different things depending on which stage the token is in.

### What should I check?
- Holder growth
- Social growth
- Large-wallet behavior
- Liquidity
- Whether new attention is still entering

### Beginner mistake
Using an early-launch strategy on a mature token.

### Takeaway
**Know what stage of the story you are trading.**

---

## Level 6 Checklist

Ask:

1. Why are people paying attention?
2. Is the narrative growing or becoming stale?
3. Is there a real catalyst?
4. Is social activity broad or concentrated?
5. Are KOLs early, late, holding, or exiting?
6. Is attention rotating into or away from this chain or theme?
